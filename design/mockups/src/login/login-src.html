Photos are muted or drained so the amber stays the one warm light. Window

The Ledger column, and beside it a window onto a desk. The photo sits in its own rounded panel on the right, inset from the edges like a card, with a slow zoom and a caption low in the frame. It is a workstation seen from above: monitor, keyboard, tablet, the tools of someone who answers things. The column keeps everything it had, desk light in the corner included.

Skyline

The company's own city behind frosted glass. A skyline at golden hour, which happens to be amber, blurred and darkened so it reads as a place rather than a picture, and the Ledger column on a glass slab in the middle. The photo drifts. It is the most "corporate" of the five and the one that would take a customer's own photo best: any office, any city.

Moves: the skyline zooms slowly behind the glass, the slab rises in. Photo: Kevin Young on Unsplash, via Lorem Picsum.

Lobby

A tall strip of building on the left, the Ledger column in the room to the right. An escalator in a lit lobby, the way into an office, panning slowly from bottom to top over a minute so the strip is never quite still. The mark and a tagline live on the strip; the column and the desk light live on the plain ground. The strip is the only colour on the page besides the amber button.

Moves: the strip pans upward, the column staggers in. Photo: Sam X on Unsplash, via Lorem Picsum.

Cover

The contained card with a photo for a cover, like a company profile. A warm desk with an orange chair across the top of the card, the mark and the desk light over it, the Ledger column underneath. The chair's orange is close enough to the amber to feel chosen. The smallest photo of the five, so the page stays mostly quiet ground, and the one that would swap for a customer's own cover most easily.

Moves: the cover zooms slowly, the card rises in. Photo: Jeff Sheldon on Unsplash, via Lorem Picsum.

Triptych

Three small pictures for the three things a desk is about. Someone at a laptop for the work, a clock for the response time, typewriter keys for the tickets, each a rounded tile floating at its own pace on the right, each labelled in one word. The clock is black and white, the other two are muted so they sit in the palette. The Ledger column takes the left.

Moves: the tiles float at three speeds and rise in one after another, the column staggers in. Photos: Alejandro Escamilla, petradr, Sergey Zolkin on Unsplash, via Lorem Picsum.
