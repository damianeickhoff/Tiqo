Tiqo Portal · Round 12

The round-11 portal with a new visual layer, and the desk queue with one rule from it. Both themes; toggle here.

What changed. Round 11 separated everything with 1px lines and gave every card the same weight; this round separates by fill instead. The page ground is a soft grey, cards are white with a low shadow and no border, and the dark theme mirrors it. The hero is the brand colour as a large field rather than ink, so the tenant's colour finally appears somewhere big. Category colour is used solid on round tiles instead of a 14% tint on a square. The type scale opens up: 46px greeting, 24px section titles, 36px page titles. The content column is 1320px on a 1440 screen.

Your three points. The maintenance notice now sits under the bar as a solid band per tone, not above it. The shelf keeps six across; the sixth slot is always Browse everything, so 5, 6 or 7 sections never leave a ragged row and an admin picks which five lead. Width is up from 1200 to 1320.

The desk. The last board is today's queue with only the fill rule applied — rail and bar on the ground, the work area as one white panel — so we can judge whether the app should follow.
