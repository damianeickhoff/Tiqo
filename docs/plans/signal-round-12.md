# Signal round 12 — the portal redesign, and the fill rule for the desk

The build plan for the round-11 and round-12 mockups, reviewed on
17 September 2026. Round 11 redrew the requester portal from a blank page;
round 12 replaced its visual layer after the first draft was judged bland
("a lot of white and grey, messy, unclear") and proved the same layer on the
desk queue. Both were approved: the portal gets the redesign, the desk gets
the layer and a tidy-up, not a redesign.

Boards: `design/mockups/boards/Portal12*.dc.html` (the layer, four screens),
`Portal11*.dc.html` (the layouts of the other seven screens, in the old
layer), `Queue12*.dc.html` (today's queue with only the layer applied). Serve
them with the `mock-preview` launch entry (port 3312). Viewers with a theme
toggle: `PortalRound12.html`, `PortalRound11.html`. Sources under
`design/mockups/src/portal/`: `css12.mjs` is the layer and is the reference
for every value below; `gen.mjs` (round 11) and `gen12.mjs` (round 12,
generated by `make12.mjs`) hold the screens.

Read [conventions.md](conventions.md) first. Drafts are saved with Save, never
on change; list-level verbs act at once.

## What was decided

- **The portal is redesigned** to the round-11 layouts in the round-12 layer.
  Every feature of today's portal is kept; the inventory that the boards were
  drawn from is in the notes on the canvas pages "Round 11 · Portal" and
  "Round 12 · Portal, by fill".
- **The desk is not redesigned.** It gets the same layer through the shared
  tokens and the `card` primitive, then a pass through every desk screen to
  drop hand-drawn lines that the fill now makes redundant. Layout, density,
  glyphs, the rail, the queue columns: unchanged.
- **Notices sit under the bar,** never above it, as solid bands: blue for
  information, amber for warning, red for outage. The approval nudge is an
  amber band in the same place.
- **The category shelf is six across.** The sixth slot is always "Browse
  everything". With five or fewer sections the shelf still shows six slots
  (empty ones collapse; the Browse tile stays last). With more than five, an
  admin picks which five lead (a `leadsPortal` position on the category, or
  simply the first five by position — the first is preferred so the choice is
  explicit). Browse everything goes to `/portal/search` with no query, which
  already lists the whole catalogue.
- **Width:** the portal column is 1320px on a 1440 screen, fluid below.
- **Two proposals accepted as drawn:** a "Where it stands" ladder on the
  request page (raised, picked up, waiting on you, resolved, with stamps) and
  a phone line on the desk card for when the desk is closed (a new setting,
  `deskPhone`, optional; the row is hidden when empty).
- **The hero's floating cards** are real data when there is any (the
  viewer's most recent open request, their most recent resolved one, the
  latest desk reply to them) and the three example cards from the board when
  there is none; the examples are marked as examples in `aria-label` only.
- The round-12 boards draw the radio dot as an oval; that is a flex-shrink
  slip in the mockup CSS. Build it round.

## The desk tidy-up (phase 1)

Counted on 17 September: 105 desk files draw their own lines
(`border-line`, `divide-line`, `border-t`, `border-b`), about 615
occurrences. The token change already softens all of them; this pass decides
each one. Rules, applied file by file:

- A line **between rows inside one card** stays (`divide-line`).
- A line **around a block** goes; the block becomes a card or a well. If it is
  the only block in its area, it needs neither.
- A line **between a header and its body** inside a card goes unless the body
  scrolls; then it stays as the scroll edge.
- A line **between sections of the ticket rail** goes; the rail-A cards from
  round 9 already contain each section.
- Toolbar and page-head edges go; the panel edge does that job now.
- Never replace a line with a stronger one.

Order: ticket page and rail, queue and saved views, settings (all panels),
form designer, projects and milestones, people, assets register and one
asset, docs, mail, change template, dashboard. Screenshot each against the
same screen before the pass; the diff should read as "fewer lines", nothing
else.

## Correction after phase 0 (17 September, evening)

Phase 0 put the desk's work area on one white panel, drawn from the queue
board. On pages made of several blocks — the ticket page, settings — that
gave white cards on a white panel. A first fix (every block its own card on
the ground, boards `Ticket13*`, `Settings13*`) was rejected on review: ten
floating rectangles on the ticket page, the round-11 problem again. The rule
that replaces it is the **surface ladder**:

- Three rungs: **ground** (the grey page), **sheet** (a white surface for one
  object), **well** (the light-grey inset inside a sheet). A block sits one
  rung above its parent, never on the same rung: no white on white, no grey
  on grey. Express it as tokens (`--ground`, `--sheet`, `--well`) and a
  component rule, not a per-page mode.
- **The main region is a sheet** by default: a ticket, a settings form, a
  register, a document are each one object. Inside a sheet, separation is
  headings, space, faint dividers and wells — the composer, a table header,
  attachments, form fields. Never a card inside a sheet.
- **Side regions sit on the ground**, so their blocks are cards: the ticket
  rail (round-9 rail A, one card per section), the settings read-out
  preview, the settings navigation with its active entry as a white pill.
- **Only genuinely composed pages opt out** of the main sheet and put cards
  on the ground: the dashboard and the project overview. The shell does this
  through one prop on those two page layouts.

Boards: `Ticket14*.dc.html` and `Settings14*.dc.html` are the chosen shape;
`Ticket13*`/`Settings13*` are kept beside them in the viewer
`design/mockups/boards/DeskFill13.html` for comparison. Apply the ladder as
the last step of phase 0, before the phase-1 pass starts, and re-verify the
four screens named there plus the dashboard.
